AI literacy & career coaching

AI can feel big. Your first step can be small.

Yesterday we asked, “What is AI?” Today we ask, “What can we do with it?” Tomorrow we’ll ask, “Okay, now what next?” My answer: AI is our cognitive twin. It thinks with you, not instead of you. And tomorrow’s twin will be smarter, more logical and more powerful than ever, so the people who learn to think with it today will lead tomorrow. I’m Umme Salma, an AI literacy and career coach with 15+ years in tech, from Samsung R&D to leading AI enablement at Bauer Media Group. I help students and professionals use AI with logic and purpose, and find their next step.

My story · Umme Salma

AI researcher, engineering leader and educator.

My path into technology started with artificial intelligence: a master's thesis on machine learning, research on neural networks, and later a research role at a German university. For 15+ years since, I have brought that thinking into engineering teams and helped people work confidently with AI.

What drives me next is helping companies transform the way they work with AI. I'm most interested in roles where engineering leadership, AI enablement and strategic leadership meet: building strong teams, guiding them through change, and turning AI from an experiment into everyday value.
